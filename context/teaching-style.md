# Teaching style (for anything Tanvi reads)

Starting rules. Update as Vikash gives feedback.

- **One idea per page.** If a page needs two big ideas, it's two pages.
- **Start from something she already knows** (a real-life comparison), then the real term.
- **Plain words.** Explain every new word the first time it appears. No acronyms without spelling them out.
- **Short.** Short sentences, short sections. A page should take ~10 minutes.
- **Do, then explain.** A small thing to try (one command, one file) beats a long explanation.
- **Show what she'll see.** Include the expected output of commands, so she knows it worked.
- **One diagram when it helps**, drawn with `/excalidraw`. Never decorative.
- **End with 2–3 tiny questions** she can answer herself, and what's next.
- **Calm tone.** Friendly, no hype, no "simply"/"just"/"obviously", no wall of emojis.
